--- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ---
A modular, secure car-rental system with a modern Tkinter GUI, SQLite3 for ACID-compliant relational data and an object-oriented design. It covers fleet management, rentals, payments and analytics.

--- KEY FEATURES ---
- Authentication and role-based access: administrator portal and customer self-service registration, with Pakistani-format validation (CNIC, mobile, email) and salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing
- Vehicle fleet management: add, edit, safe delete and search; categories such as Economy, Sedan, SUV, Luxury, Sports, Van and Hatchback; status Available / Rented / Maintenance; duplicate registration prevention
- Rental agreements: booking wizard with automatic date calculation, total = daily rate x days, security deposit kept separate from rental charges, safe database transactions
- Returns and late penalties: late charge = overdue days x daily rate; availability restored on check-in
- Digital receipts: structured invoices that can be saved as text files
- Financial ledger: payments by cash, card or bank transfer with Paid / Pending / Refunded status
- Reports: KPI dashboard (fleet, customers, active/completed rentals, revenue) and CSV exports for cars, customers, rentals, payments and revenue

--- TECH STACK ---
Python, Tkinter, SQLite3, OOP, CSV export.
