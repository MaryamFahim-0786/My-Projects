--- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ---
A secure, menu-driven banking system in Python 3 with SQLite3 and an object-oriented architecture. It has both a terminal interface and a Tkinter GUI. It follows double-entry bookkeeping principles, uses atomic transactions with rollback, PBKDF2 password hashing and exact Decimal arithmetic for money.

--- TECH STACK ---
Python 3, SQLite3, OOP with separate models, services and CLI layers, Tkinter/ttk for the GUI (no third-party GUI package), CSV export, unit tests.

--- ADMINISTRATOR PORTAL ---
- Live dashboard: customers, accounts, active/frozen/closed breakdown, total vault balance, deposits and withdrawals
- Customer lifecycle: register with Pakistani-format validation (CNIC, mobile, email, date of birth), search, update, block/unblock, safe delete
- Account operations: unique 10-digit account numbers, Savings and Current accounts, freeze/unfreeze, close (zero-balance rule)
- Transaction auditing with filters, statistical reports and one-click CSV export

--- CUSTOMER PORTAL ---
- Self-registration and secure login, profile updates, balance inquiry, deposits, withdrawals and transfers with transaction history

--- SECURITY & QUALITY ---
- Salted PBKDF2 password hashing; passwords are never stored in plain text
- Decimal arithmetic instead of floats for money
- Atomic database transactions with rollback
- Automated tests (tests/test_banking_system.py)
