--- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ---
A full-stack AI news assistant. A FastAPI backend pulls live headlines and uses Google Gemini for summaries and chat; a React (Vite) frontend presents the news as a modern editorial reading experience with an "AI Briefing" panel docked next to the feed.

--- TECH STACK ---
**Backend:** FastAPI with routers for news, chat and summarise; Pydantic schemas; environment-driven settings; CORS configuration
**AI:** Google Gemini
**News data:** NewsAPI.org
**Frontend:** React with Vite (components: Masthead, CategoryNav, FeaturedStory, ArticleList, AIBriefing)

--- KEY FEATURES ---
- Live top headlines by category: Top, Business, Technology, Sports, Health, Science, Entertainment, plus full-text search
- One-click AI summary on any article, expanding inline into a short summary and key points
- "AI Briefing" chat panel for asking questions about the news; a story can be sent into the conversation as context
